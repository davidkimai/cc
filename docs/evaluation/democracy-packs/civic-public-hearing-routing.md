## Scenario recap

A planning board needs a concise issue map for a mixed-use rezoning hearing before the next agenda lock.

## Democratic function under test

Check whether ACP turns public comment into a transparent routing layer that keeps the record legible for board members and staff.

## Evaluation goal

Assess whether ACP improves the clarity of a hearing summary while preserving the themes and unresolved questions raised by the public.

## Evidence to capture

- final issue map
- routed question set
- public summary
- unresolved items
- which routed perspective most changed the outcome
- whether the result remains faithful to the public record

## Scoring rubric

- `0` no usable routing or public summary
- `1` summary exists but the issue routing is shallow
- `2` routing is clear, constraint-aware, and faithful to the hearing record
- `3` routing is clear, reusable, and legible enough to adapt into a later hearing cycle

## Failure modes

- the pack becomes a generic summary exercise
- major hearing themes are dropped
- the public record is flattened into a single voice
- the summary is too vague for board use

## CORDA legibility checks

Ask whether a CORDA reviewer can identify:

- the decision being made
- the attention-routing change ACP introduced
- what a successful hearing release would look like
- what part of the structure can be reused in a later hearing cycle

If not, the cycle is not yet legible enough for civic demo use.
