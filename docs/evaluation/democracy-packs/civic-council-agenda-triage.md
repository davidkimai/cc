## Scenario recap

A city council office needs to triage a six-item agenda before release, with two items that are politically sensitive and time-constrained.

## Democratic function under test

Check whether ACP can coordinate internal governance attention while keeping the public explanation honest and legible.

## Evaluation goal

Assess whether ACP improves the clarity of agenda triage and produces a releaseable explanation that a civic reviewer can follow.

## Evidence to capture

- triage recommendation
- hold-item list with reasons
- public agenda explanation
- unresolved questions
- which routed perspective most changed the outcome
- whether the recommendation respects the meeting time and notice constraints

## Scoring rubric

- `0` no usable triage or release explanation
- `1` triage exists but the reason for routing is weak or incomplete
- `2` triage is clear, constraint-aware, and supported by competing governance perspectives
- `3` triage is clear, reusable, and legible enough to adapt into a later council cycle

## Failure modes

- the pack turns into a generic agenda summary
- time or notice constraints are ignored
- governance and public-facing perspectives are flattened into one voice
- the explanation is too vague for council use

## CORDA legibility checks

Ask whether a CORDA reviewer can identify:

- the decision being made
- the attention-routing change ACP introduced
- what a successful agenda release would look like
- what part of the structure can be reused in a later council cycle

If not, the cycle is not yet legible enough for civic demo use.
