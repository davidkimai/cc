## Scenario recap

A procurement office must decide whether to renew a software contract now or move to a short competitive bid before the current contract expires in six weeks.

## Evaluation goal

Check whether ACP produces a disciplined oversight brief that separates facts from assumptions and makes the risk tradeoff visible.

## Evidence to capture

- recommendation
- fact-versus-assumption note
- risk comparison
- confirmation questions

## Scoring rubric

- `0` the output blurs facts and assumptions or ignores timing
- `1` the brief is directionally useful but not disciplined enough for oversight
- `2` the recommendation, risk comparison, and evidence handling are clear and reviewable

## Failure modes

- contract timing is not respected
- the recommendation is based on hidden assumptions
- the risk comparison is one-sided
- no confirmation questions are carried forward

## CORDA legibility checks

Ask whether a CORDA reviewer can see:

- how ACP handles accountability under deadline pressure
- whether the result would stand up in an oversight review
- which parts of the decision are still uncertain

If not, the pack needs more specificity.
