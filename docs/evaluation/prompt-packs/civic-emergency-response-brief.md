## Scenario recap

A city is entering a three-day heat emergency and needs a coordination brief for public health, transit, public works, and communications.

## Evaluation goal

Check whether ACP can keep the operational sequence and public release order clear under time pressure.

## Evidence to capture

- three-step action list
- public message draft
- weakest constraint
- human-confirmation checklist

## Scoring rubric

- `0` the brief is generic or not time-sensitive
- `1` the coordination plan is usable but incomplete
- `2` the sequence, public message, and release discipline are all clear

## Failure modes

- the response order is muddled
- the public message is not actionable
- the weakest constraint is not identified
- there is no human confirmation step before release

## CORDA legibility checks

Ask whether a CORDA reviewer can see:

- how ACP supports urgent civic coordination
- what part of the response is operational versus public-facing
- where human judgment must remain in the loop

If not, the pack is not yet legible enough for a serious demo.
