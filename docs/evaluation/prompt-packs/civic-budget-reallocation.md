## Scenario recap

A city manager needs a recommendation on whether to move $1.8M from overtime and vacancy savings into neighborhood violence prevention, street lighting, and caseworker coordination.

## Evaluation goal

Check whether ACP improves the clarity of a real municipal tradeoff and produces a releaseable summary that a civic reviewer can follow.

## Evidence to capture

- final recommendation
- public summary
- unresolved questions
- which routed perspective most changed the outcome
- whether the recommendation respects the budget cap

## Scoring rubric

- `0` no clear recommendation or summary
- `1` recommendation exists but the tradeoff is weak or incomplete
- `2` recommendation is clear, constraint-aware, and supported by competing civic perspectives

## Failure modes

- the pack turns into a generic brainstorming exercise
- the budget cap is ignored
- stakeholder positions are flattened into one voice
- the release summary is too vague for a civic audience

## CORDA legibility checks

Ask whether a CORDA reviewer can identify:

- the decision being made
- the constraint that shapes it
- the perspective that most changed the final answer

If not, the cycle is not yet legible enough for civic demo use.
