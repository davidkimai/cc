# ACP Engine V2 Positioning

Status: frozen Foresight execution positioning
Owner: senior researcher / engineer
Target venue: Foresight Supercooperation, July 5, 2026

## 1. Research Object

ACP Engine V2 is the recursive constitutional deliberation engine inside ACP.

Relay remains the first implementation. Relay Blocks, SDKs, runners, and reports remain operational or proof surfaces. They do not redefine the protocol.

## 2. Central Claim

ACP coordinates attention for bounded shared reasoning under load.

Engine V2 makes that claim stronger by moving from independent heuristic ranking toward a defense-in-depth engine with:

- contribution understanding
- issue-map construction
- candidate narrowing
- recipient-conditioned judging
- set-level digest construction
- omission and fairness critics
- explanation synthesis
- confidence, abstention, and escalation signals

## 3. Venue Fit

The Foresight Supercooperation workshop asks how AI can support collective deliberation, reduce power-concentration risk, and improve democratic practice on the ground. ACP maps to that frame as open, auditable, protocol-bounded infrastructure rather than a proprietary civic copilot.

## 4. Model Policy

The production model policy is:

- `gpt-5.4-mini` for ordinary judging, extraction, critique, and explanation passes
- `gpt-5.4` for hard arbitration, benchmark gold-labeling, and difficult omission or fairness disputes

The repository must also support deterministic offline execution so demos, benchmarks, and release gates do not depend on network access. Offline execution may emulate the model-pass schemas, but it must preserve the same audit, prompt-version, and output-shape boundaries.

## 5. Non-Claims

Until stronger pilot evidence exists, do not claim:

- statistically significant democratic outcome improvement
- legal adequacy for public hearings
- consensus discovery
- representative public opinion measurement
- neutral routing
- production readiness for consumer social scale

## 6. Evidence Denominator

The flagship denominator remains `public-hearing-triage`.

Engine V2 evidence must add:

- heuristic vs recursive-engine ablation evidence
- at least one large-n public-hearing scale band beyond tiny cycles
- issue-map and critic artifacts
- limitations and non-claims suitable for paper drafting

## 7. Acceptance Criteria

Engine V2 is Foresight pre-packaging ready when:

- one routing run produces validated Engine V2 trace data
- every trace records prompt versions and model policy
- issue maps, omission warnings, fairness warnings, and escalation signals are visible in artifacts
- benchmark evidence includes an ablation summary
- scale evidence includes at least Band B and Band C public-hearing variants
- the final Foresight package includes framing, abstract, figure plan, artifact index, demo script, and limitations
